I'm in Acton today. And maybe tomorrow too, never quite sure when we will leave. Tonight we made dumplings and my contribution this time was the chili oil. 

I think it came out ok. Not the best - one time in Philly I remember Ming and Ngoc came to visit and we made pot stickers with chili oil and Ming kept going on about how good it was. You have to get the oil temperature just right to fry the chili flakes enough without burning them. 

I didn't leave the house at all today until just now when we went on a brief walk. I heard some birds in a tree and saw a frog hop across the driveway on the way back.

I'll definitely miss that part of living in Boston. Just walk anywhere, take the bus anywhere. Not always smooth or on time or enough room but it's there. In the beginning when I was still having issues walking it felt like there was too much walking. Like that one time in Fukuoka when I just wanted to get breakfast from 7-11 and spent like 2000 steps just going there and back and was like "dang what can I even do today then". Which is also why my last trip sorta went well because I brought the steam deck and was properly obsessed with Baldurs Gate 3 since I could spend time indoors with my feet up playing games without feeling like I was wasting precious time.

I am hopeful that one day we'll end up in Boston. I don't know if I will ever be able to explain the draw... like on paper it doesn't make sense. My family and friends aren't here. Boston is so expensive. My trying-new-restaurants success rate is abysmal. And yet the few things that I love here I love immensely: the river, linger, banh mi ba le. 

Ok Chicago would be excellent too. My whole life I've lived everywhere but Chicago and I know I would be in burger and hot dog and happy hour heaven. 

But I think at this point I've realized I won't find a linger type of place just anywhere. And that's important and non replaceable too.
