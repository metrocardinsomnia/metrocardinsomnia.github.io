Wednesday of our last week here. It's going to rain a lot later and this morning's run was overcast and muggy. But still good! After that first week of 90F and humid, I can't complain. I ran a Fens loop and then an MIT bridge cross and back. Did 2-3k jog + 1 min walk intervals today. Currently having some 青柳 (ao yanagi) Japanese green tea and this seven stars bakery cinnamon roll I got from George Howell. A good morning.

Monday was a lot of fun. Maybe the most fun I had in a long time. Ok maybe since Han came by the week before with jelly beans. Rose had her going away party at Trina's and I've been wanting to go since the well, last two times I was invited and couldn't make it (Old Man Bar and Rose's birthday - I think I had some excuses but in the end do they really matter?). So it felt so good to finally be living in actual Boston and able to make it to things. We got two buckets of Miller High Life ponies (thank you Andrew!), two hot dog towers, and three or so hours of talking about well I don't know, what? Big families. Shitty neighbors and mattress pickup. Cold corn and shy parents. I was really happy the whole time. I'm trying to figure out what this feels like. Maybe sophomore year spring of college? But even that was different. I do really believe (or want to believe, ha) Austin's "30s are the new 20s" but I think it is true - we're more sure of who we are, what we want, what we care about. And at this point can just do it; we've got no one to impress.

The walk back after was magical too. Inman Square to Central Square to take the 1 bus. Literally did this five years ago when I was in Cambridgeport. (Almost?) full moon on a clear Monday night with lots of people around. So many familiar Prospect St landmarks: the mosque, the Korean restaurant, the Whole Foods, Cicada. Then the donut diner and then Central Sq. I forgot how wonderful Cambridge and Somerville at night is. I wanna be there.
