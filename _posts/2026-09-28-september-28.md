I think the rain just stopped, and looking outside, it has. 

I'm trying to remember the last Nor'easter here, would've been last year, would've been more than three days of drizzle which is what we got this time. But I can't; I can't remember. 

I spent all of yesterday at home with Michelle. Cooked, napped, thought about running and highballs and what to do in Chicago next week. 

It's been a while, really. Haven't been back yet this entire year. I think we'll spend half the week at home and half the week in Evanston. 

I want to run on the lake. I want to get a polish sausage. I want Red Hot Ranch and at least one sorta nice sitdown meal. 

I want to accept, and I think I have accepted, that I am not going to run Cambridge Half in November. And I am not going to do Osaka Marathon in February even though I could. Because somehow 18 months later I still am not back to fully healthy running. 

I realized in San Francisco that I just want to be able to do exploration runs again. Downtown to the Presidio, out and back on the Golden Gate Bridge, cliffside trails to Land's End. I want to get off the commuter rail here in Wellesley and run the Boston route and then along the river and finish up at the Speedway where I always want to be. That's what I want. I want that more than most anything right now and certainly more than any amount of races and PRs and whatever else external. 

So we're gonna stop. Breathe. Run some beaches in Evanston. Maybe that big hill and the worn out track at Montrose Harbor. Definitely some Lincoln Park and as much of the Lakefront Trail as I can. Healthily.
