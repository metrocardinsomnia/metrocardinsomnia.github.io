Mornings are getting colder. Around 50F today. Having a weird slow week this week and amongst it all have e this feeling I should be doing something else.

Which is to say I need to go into Boston. 

But also realizing I want to change a few things. Nothing drastic. Maybe adjust is a better word.

Less wifi, less work, less stress, less alc. 

More sleep, more tea, more Boston, more time with Michelle, more cooking, more reading, more Yotsuba before bed.

More walking and less running? Fewer things and more things I love? More writing. I should write something about San Francisco. I think I wrote on the plane there but on the plane back my head and stomach hurt and I couldn't do anything. 

Cambridge Half is in less than two months and even more so now I worry about whether I can do it safely. If you're not sure don't do it. Don't rush it. Take your time and focus on what's real and before you: days, weeks, runs, walks. 

There have been good things lately. Last round of dumplings, our filling was perfect. Eddworks suji. Reading Yotsuba before bed and no snacks after 8pm. Nighttime walks, seeing stars. Stardew skull cavern. Making cold brew tea as a last steep so I get two types of tea each day. Healthy home food for lunch so I'm not just eating hotdogs and frozen pizza. San Francisco. Family time. Colder mornings, cooler evenings. Salmon sorta hongshao style with ginger and soy sauce and mirin. Yogurt bowls. Radiko. Thinking about the future, a future where maybe we live in Boston and don't need a car and on a night like this I could just go somewhere in the evening on a whim.
