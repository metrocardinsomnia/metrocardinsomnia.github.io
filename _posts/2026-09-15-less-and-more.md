Mornings are getting colder. Around 50F today. Having a weird slow week this week and amongst it all have e this feeling I should be doing something else.

Which is to say I need to go into Boston. 

But also realizing I want to change a few things. Nothing drastic. But less wifi, less work, less stress, less alc. 

More sleep, more tea, more Boston, more time with Michelle, more cooking, more reading, more Yotsuba before bed.

More walking and less running? Fewer things and more things I love? More writing. I should write something about San Francisco. I think I wrote on the plane there but on the plane back my head and stomach hurt a lot and I couldn't do anything. 

Cambridge Half is in less than two months and still I feel doubt about whether I can do it. If you're not sure don't do it. Don't rush it. Take your time and focus on what's real and before you: days, weeks, runs, walks.
