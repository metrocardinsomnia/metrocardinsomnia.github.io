Down to our last week in Boston. My foot is doing better; back up to 3 runs per week, 4 rest days. Tried out new places I've had bookmarked for a long time: Billy's Sub Shop, Aatma, Brassica, Berryline. Spending time at the speedway, on the esplanade, and well of course, not in the Worcester area. 

Perhaps the best things about Boston life is the absence of Worcester things: car worries, ~2 hour commuter rails, slow suburbanites who don't know how to drive nor navigate a grocery store. 

It is so refreshing how this area - Fens and Mass Ave - is so much younger and more diverse. Feels like college again with window AC and laundry in the basement and people playing frisbee by the soccer field and walking around everywhere. Well the irony is that even though it feels like I'm finally living around people "my age" I'm probably now older than most of them even though I still feel like five years younger. Ok to be fair when I was in Japan three months ago multiple people guessed I was a college student so...

Japanese has been going well. I think I've stalled at around 1650 kanji and am just trying to maintain the daily ~100 kanji reviews. Earlier this week - Monday and Tuesday - I was falling behind with 300+ since I was just doing work all day and my eyes and brain were too fried at night to do Japanese. But that's fine. We will still get to 2000 eventually but no hurry - I've been redirecting energy to Discord Japanese chat instead. Just read all the messages going on and try to respond every now and the. Learn what people use. Figuring out replies is hard too but that is what makes you better - understanding questions and formulating answers. Who cares if your grammar is correct; just say something back. 

Yesterday I ran ~50 minutes or so doing 2k jog 1 min walk. Plan for next week is just 3-4 runs once again, keeping total volume under 30k (20% more than this week). Under 28k would be even better. 

For the rest of the year, we should do a mandatory "injury reminder day" of Xk jog + 1' walk intervals so you physically remember what it's like to be recovering from injury. Otherwise it's too too easy for you to completely forget and go back to cranking more miles and workouts and comparing yourself to people.

Plan for today. Just got back from Brassica. Konya is on her way back and then I'll go to the speedway (Rose's last shift!!) and then for dinner we can do rice + chicken/bok choy + maybe tomato egg or something else. Tofu could work too.
