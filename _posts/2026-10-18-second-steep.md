It's cold now. Frost on the car this morning. Yesterday I opened up my winter clothes suitcase to get my gloves and hats and running tights out.

Just last weekend it was 81 degrees in Chicago and I was getting mosquito bites waiting for the 73 bus to Red Hot Ranch. And now it's back to fall feeling not too far from winter.

Today I'm at home writing this even though normally I'm supposed to be at Linger and Miche is supposed to be running somewhere fun like on the Charles River or Bruce Freeman and certainly not here in Worcester. But last weekend we came back to not just fall but also car issues once again (power steering fluid leak this time) so it's a stay-at-home weekend after all.

But it's okay and we're going to still have a good day because today's good day began with a morning walk outside calling my Dad and thinking about stuff to write about. And then coming back home to sheng puer tea from mom and some new old Stevie Wonder from 50 years ago ("It's Easier", specifically) thanks to jwave midday lounge playing this a few weeks ago. 

How about I write about some good things lately.

Chicago. Oh Chicago, what a wonderful trip especially since somehow I have not been back this whole calendar year. I knew this all along but only just acknowledged that my favorite food is Chicago food. Burgers, fries, hot dogs, beer. Pita Inn business lunch special. Masa pancakes with mole syrup from Dove's Luncheonette. Happy hour. Well, happy hour isn't a Chicago thing only but they don't have it here in Mass and that's messed up and even the Philly happy hours had nothing on Chicago. Burgers coming with fries. Little Bad Wolf, Gretel, Red Hot Ranch. So many good places. Pulkit and Konya's party. Sugar Moon with Chris and Michelle on a Friday morning eating our box together in the park: wow that harvest apple filled croissant thing was so good. If it was just apple and cinnamon it'd be no different from a turnover but the hazelnut and cream gave it so much depth. Hanging out with friends and mom and dad and Big Smo at home. Running along the lakefront trail and Northwestern Campus and taking the buses and L and metra and wow all the transportation is so much better than here and cheap! That $5 unlimited 24 hour pass saved us so much money and you don't have to think about transfers or anything else. Same thing would be $11 in Boston absolutely ridiculous, really. 

Planning my next Japan trip in February: Nagasaki for sara udon and bay views and mountainside exploring, Fujikawaguchiko for Mt. Fuji views and lake perimeter runs a la Yuru Camp, Nara for a ci-ca IC card and the deer and temples and Sakai city pit stop for Ashi Hamono and Konosuke because I still want more knives even though I have too many knives already. I don't want to go too crazy but will try to make one reservation for a fancy sushi place in Hakata, thinking Sushi Gosuian or Zaisho and probably a Sushi to Shuko Tomikawa redemption visit in Kichijoji (assuming I'm not banned from there from last time when I had to bail halfway through due to Sendai yakitori food poisoning the night before...). I want to drink a lot of sake haha. I wanna go back to Okayama to say hi to Ai-chan and go to kai-fork for kanzake (warm sake) especially since it'll be winter and I love how they serve everything in local bizen yaki ceramicware (which I really need to get this time last time it was raining and I didn't want to take the train to Imbe but there's at least a few places in Okayama city itself).

Trying to figure out what sakes I want to hunt down and possibly bring back. Ubusuna of course which is still my favorite. Nichi Nichi and Koshimeijo? Gotta bring some bubble wrap and maybe buy a hardshell suitcase at Muji or something at Tokyo then as a checked bag. But anyways current iteration of my itinerary is Tokyo -> Nagasaki -> Hakata -> Okayama -> Sakai/Nara -> Wakayama -> Hamamatsu (for Genkotsu hamburg) -> Fujikawaguchiko -> Tokyo. Not super sure about the Wakayama. And I do want to go to Fukui and Toyama and Niigata and Akita eventually too but maybe better for the fall? If all goes well with running I'm thinking Kochi or Osaka marathon 2028 February. 

Speaking of. Running and walking have been good again this week. Finally understanding (maybe) that I'm on like a two or three week delay where my feeling good now is thanks to me taking it easy two weeks ago. And it is not an ok to start blasting volume and workouts because then in two weeks from now, my foot will be back to feeling bad. 

So going into next week it's just a simple 10% bump to 36-37k total. The week after can either be 40k flat or a deload. This week we did three ~50 minute runs and one 25 minute haircut run so next week I think we just add another short run and that should be enough. Don't need any longer long runs yet. Can literally just be running to the Worcester commuter rail and back Saturday and Sunday depending on how the car is doing and if there's a bus. We'll be fine.

I don't know exactly what I've been doing this whole time but have been using my Kagekiyo 210mm aogami gyuto a lot this past week and have been loving it. I've just been too scared to use it because like all things I buy, if things are "too nice" I get way too OCD and am scared of using it. But once I get over it, it's good.
