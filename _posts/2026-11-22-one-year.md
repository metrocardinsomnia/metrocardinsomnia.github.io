We're in Philly again, one year later. Miche had a good race today and a good race last year.

As for me. Well in person I don't really want to talk about it, honestly. But writing is different and that's why I like it even when I don't have good things to report.

I don't know if I've made any real progress this past year. Foot still gets wonky from too much walking, too much running. I wouldn't say I'm depressed but I would say I'm disappointed, have been disappointed for a long time. 

What did I do right? Last year after this trip I did a second MRI that came back good and then two to three months of PT. PT was good though twice a week was dumb and expensive. Nothing really changes between Tuesday and Thursday and I'm just paying money to do stretches and run on a treadmill. Still a net positive experience though and still doing single leg squats and calf raises and RDLs and stuff on my own.

May and June were good. Great. July was bad. August and September and October were all figuring things out again. 

Where do I see myself in a year? Have I even thought that far ahead or have I only been living within the week ahead and the week past?

If there's only one thing I could have I want to be doing healthy running again. That's my job, treat it like your job.

Real job's not even that important compared to that. 

Ok so healthy running in one year. Living somewhere good with Miche in one year. Continuing Japanese, calling my parents after dinner, making time for tea and slower living.

You have to recognize that it's somehow easier to want more. It's hard to want less. But we need it. 

Less running. Less work. Less stress.
